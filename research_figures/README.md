# Selected research figures

This folder contains a selection of figures that I generated at different
points throughout the research. It is not meant to contain every figure or
every result I produced. I mainly kept figures that represented a different
experiment, question, or stage in how my analysis developed.

The folders are arranged roughly in the order that the research progressed:

- `01_taxonomy_formation` contains figures from my first larger Gemma analysis,
  including the early behavioural taxonomy and comparisons with answer-only
  prompting.
- `02_monitorability_pilots` contains figures from the point where I started
  looking beyond the behavioural taxonomy and experimenting with sampling,
  candidate scoring, source masking, trace visibility, and prediction around
  the answer boundary.
- `03_gemma_final_case_study` contains the later Gemma analysis where I started
  bringing the behavioural and diagnostic results together.
- `04_cross_model_expansion` contains figures from extending the experiments
  beyond Gemma to GPT-OSS and Qwen and comparing how the three models behaved.
- `05_deep_research_synthesis` contains selected figures from the wider
  hypothesis testing and cross-diagnostic analysis I carried out while deciding
  which results were strong enough and relevant enough to keep.
- `06_final_dissertation` contains the final versions of the figures that I
  selected and redrew for the dissertation.

Some of the earlier figures show experiments or interpretations that I later
refined, qualified, or decided not to include in the final dissertation. I
have kept them because they show the different questions I investigated and
how my understanding of the results changed as the research progressed. The
final dissertation figures in `06_final_dissertation` should be treated as
the final versions of the results I ultimately reported.
